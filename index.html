How it works

  1. 1
    You give. Once, or a few dollars a month.
  2. 2
    It becomes permanent capital. Placed in Ukrainian government bonds. The capital itself is never spent.
  3. 3
    Only the yield is paid out. Every month, to pensioners — starting at 1,000 UAH per person, designed to grow over time.

You don't get anything back — this is a donation, not a financial product. What lasts forever is the capital, not a guaranteed lifetime payment to any one person.
