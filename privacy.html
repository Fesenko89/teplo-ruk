Warm Hands Teplo Ruk

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who we are

Warm Hands (Teplo Ruk) is an early-stage initiative to create a permanent charitable fund for Ukrainian pensioners. The charity is not registered yet. Until it is, the person responsible for your data (the data controller) is the founder, Mykola Fesenko. Contact: .

What we collect

  • Required: your email, country and the monthly amount you plan to give.
  • Optional: your employer, whether you're open to discussing a larger or company gift, and how you heard about us.
  • Referral data: your personal invite code, and the code of the person who invited you, if any.
  • The date of your sign-up and whether you confirmed it by email.

We do not collect payment details — we don't accept money at this stage. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies. Your browser stores the invite code you arrived with (local storage) so it can be attached to your sign-up.

Why

  • To find out whether there is enough interest to register the charity (the goal is 150–300 confirmed sign-ups in 6 weeks).
  • To show the total number of confirmed sign-ups on the site (a number only, no personal data).
  • To email you once if the fund launches, so you can decide whether to give.
  • Your employer helps us estimate whether corporate gift-matching could apply.

The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time.

Where it is stored

Sign-ups are stored in a Google Sheet in the founder's Google account, and confirmation emails are sent through Gmail. Google may process this data outside the EU, including in the United States. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it

If the charity is not registered after this test, all sign-up data is deleted within 30 days of the project closing. If it is registered, we keep your data only until we've contacted you about the launch, and no longer than 12 months from your sign-up, unless you choose to stay in touch.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by emailing . We will do it within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
